2024 reference fleet, not live status.

54 commercial plant sites and 94 reactors. Retired, suspended/out-of-service, planned, construction and research reactors are excluded. All included sites are in the contiguous U.S.

Power

Net summer electrical capacity in MW(e), as published for 2024. This is capability, not current production, annual generation or thermal power. 1,000 MW = 1 GW.

Dates

Commercial-operation month/year for each included reactor. A plant’s date range covers its oldest through newest included unit. Color represents the oldest included unit, not its last expansion. Retired units at the same site are excluded.

Groups and filters

Outlined, numbered circles group nearby sites. In Capacity mode their area represents combined capacity; in Opening dates mode they remain neutral because they contain multiple dates. Select a group to choose a plant or zoom in.

The year filter selects reactors in this reference fleet that opened by the chosen year; their power stays at 2024 capacity. The histogram always shows all 94 reference reactors, not the filtered population or historical fleet size.
